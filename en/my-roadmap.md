# My RoadMap

## Which path to choose?

WWhatever branch of cybersecurity you want to dedicate yourself to, there are different certifications.

In my case, I'm specializing in offensive security: it's the branch I enjoy and the one I already work in day to day. So here's the path I'm following, based on my experience and on what I've seen in people who hold the kind of roles I'm aiming for.

I'll also share my experience as I pass each certification, and whether I think it's worth it or not.

There is a roadmap of certifications divided by branches that can serve as a guide: [https://pauljerimy.com/security-certification-roadmap/](https://pauljerimy.com/security-certification-roadmap/)

## Certification Roadmap

This roadmap isn't set in stone: I adjust it based on what I learn, what my job needs, and advice from colleagues in the field.
