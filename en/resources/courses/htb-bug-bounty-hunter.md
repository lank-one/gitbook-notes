# HTB Bug Bounty Hunter

For [HTB Certified Bug Bounty Hunter](https://academy.hackthebox.com/preview/certifications/htb-certified-bug-bounty-hunter) (CBBH) certification, there is a path in the HackTheBox academy.

To access it, go to [academy.hackthebox.com](https://academy.hackthebox.com/) and log in to your HackTheBox account.

Once on the dashboard, in the side menu on the left, open the Path > Job Role Paths drop-down menu and search for the Bug Bounty Hunter path.

Click on the Enroll button and the different modules will appear. In this case, this job role path consists of 20 modules, each of which has submodules.

The modules explain different parts of a web application, including theory, questions, and small labs with practical exercises.

Each module has a cost in cubes, the platform's currency, and until you complete 100% of the course modules, the purchase of the voucher to take the certification exam will not be unlocked. As you complete activities within each module, you will be refunded part of the cubes that you will spend on unlocking each module.

If you don't have cubes, which can be obtained by completing machines or free training courses within the Academy platform, the best strategy would be as follows:
